What we do

Complete care for the trees around your home

From one overgrown limb to a yard full of mature trees, every job gets an arborist’s eye: what to cut, what to keep, and what your trees need to stay healthy for decades.

  • Pruning & trimming

    Careful cuts that strengthen structure, clear roofs and lines, and lighten limbs before wind and snow. Includes crown thinning and reduction.

    Safe, controlled removal of dead, dying, or hazardous trees, planned around your home, fences, and landscaping.

    Help choosing the right tree for the right spot, planted properly so it settles in and thrives.

    Diagnosis and treatment for the insects and diseases that weaken trees, plus ongoing care to keep them vigorous.

Why it matters

Why hire a certified arborist, not just a tree guy

Anyone can cut limbs. An ISA Certified Arborist knows how a tree will respond to every cut, how to spot disease and decay early, and when a tree can be saved instead of removed. Certification means passing the International Society of Arboriculture’s exam and keeping up with continuing education.

That knowledge protects your trees, and your property, for the long haul.

  • Pruning cuts that heal well and guide healthy growth
  • Early warning on disease, pests, and structural problems
  • Straight answers on when to save a tree and when to remove it

Why prune your trees?

Regular pruning keeps trees strong, safe, and healthy. Along the Wasatch Front that matters: a thinned crown lets canyon winds pass through instead of catching the tree like a sail, and lighter limbs shed heavy, wet snow instead of snapping under it. Good pruning also corrects weak structure early, so a tree lives longer and looks better.

Tree health assessments: catch problems early

Not sure if a tree is thriving or in trouble? A professional assessment can find disease, pests, decay, root problems, or structural weakness before they turn costly or dangerous. We’ll walk you through the tree’s condition and recommend the best steps to keep it strong.

FAQ

Common questions

Are estimates free?

Yes. We’ll look at your trees, talk through what you want, and give you a clear recommendation and price, with no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. The Tree Amigos is licensed and insured, with an ISA Certified Arborist on staff.

When is the best time to prune?

For most trees, late winter while they’re dormant: cuts close faster and the tree’s structure is easy to see without leaves. Dead, broken, or hazardous limbs should come off as soon as possible, any time of year.

Can a sick tree be saved?

Often, if the problem is caught early. A health assessment tells you what’s wrong, whether treatment is likely to work, and what it will take. If a tree can’t be saved, we’ll tell you that too.
